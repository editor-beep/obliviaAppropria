# Oblivia Appropria — Detailed Story Outline

## 1) OBA-001 — Rift Silence (`rift-silence`)
- **Setting / route:** JFK → NBO, East African Rift lakeside town.
- **Surface case:** Flamingos have disappeared.
- **Underlying crime:** Saline mineral extraction damage hidden as “regenerative stewardship.”
- **Corporate actor(s):** Meridian Saline Ventures + Luxembourg/Zurich finance chain.
- **Cover-up narrative:** Ecological healing/regeneration language reframes extraction as stewardship.
- **Local help:** Akinyi (lawyer), Omondi (fisherman), Joseph (driver), Alexandrei (journalist).
- **Core dramatic image:** Livestreamed leaking gasket triggers board-level panic.
- **Meta function in arc:** Establishes pattern: mystical framing vs extractive reality.
