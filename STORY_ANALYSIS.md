## How the collection works (the shared formula)

Every story is a variation on one engine, and you can't judge originality without naming it:

1. A corporation or state contractor commits a material crime (extraction, trafficking, weapons testing, theft, fraud) **hidden behind wellness / sustainability / spiritual / "regenerative" branding**.
2. A whistleblower or witness has already died or vanished; the death is officially ruled an **accident** ("cardiac event of undetermined origin," "a fall," "traumatic industrial accident of undetermined origin").
3. The perpetrator retains **Oblivia Appropria** of *Appropria Somatic Forensics LLC* for a "vibrational / auric / atmospheric assessment" (almost always **$50,000**; **$30,000** domestically) to spiritually launder the site.
4. Oblivia, **completely oblivious**, produces the one piece of evidence that cracks the case — usually by livestreaming geotagged footage to her **612,000 followers**, photographing something with GPS-enabled metadata, or physically blundering into proof.
5. A **competent local woman "who has been correct for a long time"** (a litigator, scientist, coordinator, or kaitiaki) weaponizes that evidence into a regulatory/criminal collapse.
6. Oblivia's sidekick **Alexandrei (A. Harris)** closes with a deadpan *Field Notes — Final Disposition* casefile, always including the running gag of a **$95 airport souvenir** logged as a "consecrated fragment," and the signature dual verdict:
   **`CASE CLOSED (SUBJECTIVE) / [criminal status] (MATERIAL)`**.

A recurring conspiratorial thread ties many cases together: **Meridian Impact Partners** and its "impact fund ecosystem" (named in Senegal, Nepal, Iowa, and adjacent to the Standing Rock and Sedona liability counsel). Alexandrei flags it in his ledger across stories.

**The satire therefore runs on four reusable instruments**, and each story dials them up or down:
- **(A) The retainer document** — corporate euphemism ("atmospheric disturbance following a heritage transition," "the scope is spiritual… and shall produce no legally discoverable findings").
- **(B) Oblivia's mystical misreadings** — dramatic irony (the bleaching silk is "the lake undying my dress"; an active illegal logging clearing is "the forest's sacred ceremony of return").
- **(C) A. Harris's casefile deadpan** — the $95-souvenir gag and the SUBJECTIVE/MATERIAL binary.
- **(D) Influencer-culture mockery** — hashtags, follower counts, "slow fashion" totes.

**Scope note / correction:** there are **17 distinct story files** (excluding `index.ts`). An earlier inventory claimed `rift-silence` and `certified-forest` were duplicates sharing an "Ahmad Rifai / GPS-spoofing" plot — **this is incorrect**. `rift-silence` (Kenya) is a saline-surfactant chemical-reveal story whose dead assessor is *Soren Vance-Vanderbilt*; `certified-forest` (Borneo) is the GPS-spoofing logging story whose dead ranger is *Ahmad Rifai*. They are distinct. The genuine near-twins in the set are identified below (the chemical-stain trio, and the EM-meter pair). The `*-tbd.ts` files (`fashion-tbd`, `tech-tbd`) are finished stories despite their draft-style filenames.

## Satire scale used below

- **① Muted / Elegiac** — irony is present but restrained; the gravity of the subject dominates.
- **② Moderate** — steady dramatic irony plus gentle euphemism-mockery.
- **③ High** — sharp, sustained satire across all four instruments at once.
- **④ Biting / Farce** — overt comedy, caricature, meta-commentary.

# Part I — International cases (OBA-001 → OBA-011)

## 1. Rift Silence — `rift-silence.ts` (OBA-001) · Lake Elementaita, Kenya
**Industry:** "regenerative" saline/soda-ash mineral extraction (Meridian Saline Ventures).

**Main plot points**
- 400,000 lesser flamingos have vanished from Lake Elementaita; the "pink frequency" is gone. Compliance assessor **Soren Vance-Vanderbilt** flagged irregular pH and brine overflow, was denied filtration equipment ("aesthetic disruption to the regenerative campus"), and died of neurotoxic exposure filed as a "cardiac event."
- Meridian retains Oblivia to spiritually paper over his "acute somatic detachment." Water-rights litigator **Akinyi Omoro** waits at the perimeter holding Soren's water-damaged field notes.
- Oblivia livestreams a "Mineral Communion," wading in with her grandmother's silk wrap. The silk **bleaches** on camera — a chemical reaction front. Water chemist **Dr. Fatuma Wanjiku** recognizes it as industrial sulfosuccinate discharge (not natural soda-ash) and tweets it; NEMA issues an emergency order.
- Sampling confirms surfactant at 4,000× safe levels plus the compound that killed Soren. ICC referral; the Zurich parent fund's sustainability cert suspended.

**Satire level: ③ High.** The establishing entry; it sets every instrument — the retainer's "no legally discoverable findings," the bleaching silk as dramatic irony, the $95 soda-ash disc, "Case closed."

**Originality notes:** Strong, evocative locale (the *subtraction* of sound) and the "body/fabric as chemical test-strip → remote expert diagnoses via social media" reveal. But this reveal becomes the template for **two siblings** (`sacred-water`, `poisoned-healing`), which costs it distinctiveness. Supporting cast (Akinyi) is the prototype of the recurring "correct-for-a-long-time woman."

## 2. Sacred Water — `sacred-water.ts` (OBA-002) · Franschhoek / Stellenbosch, South Africa
**Industry:** "mineral-terroir" wine retreat fronting illegal lithium-carbonate extraction (Lithium-Logic Holdings).

**Main plot points**
- Influencer **Kezia van der Merwe** went "dark" after incoherent videos with hand tremors; the estate calls it a "voluntary digital detox residency."
- Undisclosed lithium wells contaminate the water table; the "lithium-enriched natural wine" carries toxic doses. Estate manager **Gary Oosterhout** is bound by a silence clause.
- **Variation on the formula:** Oblivia herself becomes the poisoned body. Drinking the wine, she develops intention tremor and facial dysesthesia, which she narrates as "ascending / a somatic purge."
- Her sister **Philippa**, a Boston hospitalist, watches the livestream and phones with the correct diagnosis (lithium toxicity, 2.4 mEq/L — she was briefly in real danger). Kezia is found, recovers; a suppressed 2020 internal assessment ("acceptable for product differentiation") surfaces.

**Satire level: ③ High.** "I think I might be ascending," she said, with less confidence than usual" is the book's tonal signature.

**Originality notes:** Best **structural twist** of the chemical-stain trio — the detective is the test subject, and the expert is a *relative* on the phone rather than a stranger on Twitter. Still, mechanistically it's the second of three "tox-symptoms → remote clinician" stories.
